The job scheduler in this repo (job, graph, scheduler, report) has several bugs. (1) A dependency cycle, including a job that depends on itself, is never reported: Scheduler(...) should raise ValueError mentioning 'cycle' as the docstring in graph.py says. (2) When a job fails for good, only its direct dependents are skipped; their dependents should be skipped too, all the way down, whatever order the jobs were registered in. (3) Jobs of equal priority should run in name order, but they run in registration order. (4) The wait before a retry is twice as long as documented (backoff * 2**(n-1) after the n-th failure). (5) run_all gives up too early: a job that is waiting out its backoff is still pending, so run_all must keep ticking until it has run. Find and fix all five.
