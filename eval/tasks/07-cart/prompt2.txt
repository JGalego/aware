Add coupon support. Create coupons.py with a COUPONS dict mapping a code to (percent_off, last_valid_date) with dates as 'YYYY-MM-DD' strings, and add Cart.apply_coupon(code, today) where today is also a 'YYYY-MM-DD' string. A coupon is valid up to and including its last date; an unknown or expired code raises ValueError; a second coupon replaces the first. total() applies the coupon percent to the already-discounted lines total, rounded half up, and receipt() shows a COUPON line, before TOTAL, with the amount taken off as a negative number.
