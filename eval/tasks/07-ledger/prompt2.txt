Add period closing. Ledger.close_period(date, retained='Retained Earnings') posts one transaction dated `date`, with the memo 'closing entries', that brings every income and expense account to zero as of that date (a line of minus its raw balance, in the account's own currency, skipping accounts already at zero) and puts the balancing amount on the retained earnings account, a line in the base currency equal to minus the sum of the other lines converted to the base currency at the closing date. It returns that transaction, or None (posting nothing) if there was nothing to close. The retained earnings account must exist (KeyError otherwise) and be in the base currency (ValueError otherwise). Afterwards the ledger is closed through `date`, available as Ledger.closed_through (None before the first close): post() with a date on or before it raises ValueError('period closed'), and so does a close_period whose date is on or before it, even when it had nothing to close. Later periods start from the closed books, so a second close only moves what happened since.
