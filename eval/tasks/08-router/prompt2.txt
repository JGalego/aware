Add mounting. Router.mount(prefix, sub, name_prefix='') copies the routes and middleware that sub has at that moment into the router, with prefix put in front of every pattern and name_prefix in front of every route name, so url_for works through mounts (url_for('api.user', id=3) for a route named 'user' mounted with name_prefix='api.' under '/api/v1'). prefix must start with '/' and must not end with '/' (ValueError otherwise). A mounted router's own middleware apply only to its routes, inside the parent's middleware (parent first, then the sub-router's, then deeper mounts' in the same order), and the parent's own routes never see them. Mounts nest. Routes added to sub after the mount are not part of it. Matching rules (fewest parameters, 405 with Allow, the trailing-slash 308) work across mounted and local routes alike. Route names must be unique: add and mount raise ValueError('duplicate route name <name>') instead of silently shadowing one.
