Add two functions to sales.py. with_change(totals) takes the dict from monthly_totals and returns a list of (month, total, pct_change) tuples in order, where pct_change is the change from the previous month as a percentage rounded to 1 decimal place (None for the first month, and None when the previous total is 0). best_months(csv_text, n=3) returns the n highest months as (month, total) tuples, highest first, ties broken by the earlier month.
